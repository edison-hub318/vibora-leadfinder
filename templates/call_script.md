## Opener
"Hi, is that the owner? It's {my_name} from Vibora, a small web design studio just down the road. I was looking at {trade} in {town} on Google and noticed something I thought you'd want to know. Have you got two minutes?"

## The hook
- Mention the specific problem from the hook line (no website / Facebook only / not mobile-friendly).
- "You've got great reviews, but anyone who searches for you can't see much beyond that."

## Questions
1. Where do most of your new customers come from at the moment?
2. Do people ever say they couldn't find you online?
3. If you had a simple site that brought in a few more calls a month, would that be worth a look?

## The ask
"I'd love to show you a quick mock-up I can put together for free. Could we do 15 minutes on Thursday or Friday?"

## If they say no
Thank them, ask if it's OK to check back in a few months, and log the objection.
